# 03 — Principal’s One-Page Launch Brief

**Title**: Beginning the Shift — From Industrial Schooling to Human Formation (2026–2030 Pilot)

**Why now**  
The world our students will inhabit is changing faster than our current school design. Cheap cognitive tools and autonomous systems are already reshaping work. Continuing to optimize mainly for compliance, coverage, and traditional credentials risks leaving young people under-prepared for judgment, agency, relationships, and meaning. We can start small and carefully.

**What we are not doing**  
- Abolishing teachers or schools.  
- Replacing human relationships with AI.  
- Throwing out all structure or accountability overnight.  
- Imposing a single ideology or commercial platform.

**What we are doing**  
- Running voluntary, time-bounded pilots that protect childhood social development while modernizing how learning is organized and assessed.  
- Elevating teachers as mentors and designers.  
- Teaching students to use powerful tools without surrendering their minds.  
- Measuring what actually forms capable, grounded human beings.

**First 90 days**  
1. Leadership + volunteer staff complete the Diagnosis Sheet.  
2. Form a small redesign team that includes students and parents.  
3. Choose one grade band or 1–2 classrooms for Phase 1 (AI as tutor support under strict human oversight).  
4. Establish privacy, consent, and “human non-negotiables” rules.  
5. Communicate openly with the whole community.

**Success looks like**  
Students who can learn with machines and without them; teachers who feel their professional judgment is valued; a school culture that treats every child as a future citizen and human being, not primarily as future labor.

**Ask of the board / community**  
Permission to pilot, transparent reporting, and the courage to iterate based on evidence rather than fashion.
