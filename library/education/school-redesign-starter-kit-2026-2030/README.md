# School Redesign Starter Kit (2026–2030)
## A practical pilot pack for principals, teachers, and communities

**Status**: Living project under Polymath-HQ / Humanity in the Machine Age  
**Created**: 2026-10-02  
**Purpose**: Give any school leader a concrete, low-risk way to begin shifting from an industrial labor-preparation model toward a system that forms whole human beings — judgment, character, creativity, relationships, agency, and the capacity to think with and without machines.

### Core conviction

The worldwide industrial-era school model was optimized for producing reliable workers for factories, offices, and bureaucracies. That model is now mismatched to a world of cheap cognition, agentic software, and rapid task automation.  
We do not need more schools that primarily sort and train labor. We need institutions that cultivate human beings who can live with dignity, purpose, and agency when many traditional jobs change or disappear.

This kit is **not** a call to abolish schools or teachers. It is a call to redesign the purpose, daily experience, assessment, and adult roles inside schools so they serve human development first.

### What this kit contains (v0.1 skeleton)

1. **Diagnosis sheet** — quick audit of current school against industrial vs human-formation goals.  
2. **2026–2030 pilot phases** — sequenced, low-risk experiments a principal can run.  
3. **Role redesign** — teachers as mentors/designers/guardians; AI as tutor & co-designer.  
4. **Mastery & demonstration model** — replace pure seat-time + high-stakes exams with competency evidence.  
5. **Human non-negotiables** — what must never be outsourced (friendship, play, conflict resolution, physical presence, moral reasoning practice).  
6. **AI literacy + epistemic literacy curriculum outline**.  
7. **Privacy & lifelong learning record guardrails**.  
8. **Community & parent engagement template**.  
9. **Equity & low-infrastructure adaptations** (Global South / rural / low-connectivity).  
10. **Measurement that matters** — human flourishing indicators, not only test scores or college placement.  
11. **Risk register & exit ramps** — how to pilot without harming students if experiments fail.  
12. **One-page “Principal’s Launch Brief”** — ready to share with staff and board.

### Design principles

- Human formation first; workforce readiness second (and redefined).  
- Preserve childhood social development.  
- Teachers remain essential; their role elevates.  
- AI is a powerful tutor and tool, never a replacement for human relationship.  
- Start small, measure what matters, iterate, keep exit ramps.  
- Equity is not optional — designs must work in low-resource settings.  
- Every change must enlarge student agency, not just efficiency.

### Relationship to the book

This kit operationalizes the education chapters of *Humanity in the Machine Age* (especially the 2026–2030 transition scenario and the human-blueprint principles). It is developed as a separate living project so it can be tested, refined, and used by real schools while the book is written.
