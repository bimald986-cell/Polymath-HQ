## Core conviction

The industrial-era school model was optimized to produce reliable workers. In an age of cheap cognition and agentic systems, schools should form whole human beings: judgment, character, agency, relationships, creativity, and the capacity to think with and without machines. Workforce readiness remains important but secondary to human formation.

Teachers stay essential (elevated role: mentor, designer, guardian). Childhood social development is non-negotiable. Pilots must be low-risk, equity-first, and reversible.
