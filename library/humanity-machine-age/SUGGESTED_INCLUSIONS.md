## 5. Synthetic media & epistemic security

## 6. Care economy & human presence premium

- Sharpen the economic case: genuine human presence, mentorship, and emotional labor may become more valuable (and better paid) precisely because cognitive/physical tasks get cheaper.

## 8. Forecast audit prominence

- Make Appendix O (“What would prove us wrong?”) more prominent — a living confidence dashboard with explicit falsifiers and signposts for every major Polymath forecast.

## 9. Consciousness / moral status methodology

- Short methodological note: performance ≠ experience; other-minds problem; risks of both over- and under-attribution of sentience. Keep current caution.
