## Editorial compass

This book is optimistic about the possibility that AI and machines can enlarge human flourishing, but optimism is a hypothesis to test, not a conclusion to assume. The manuscript must challenge its own forecasts and clearly distinguish evidence from inference.

Important terminology: current AI systems are not established to be conscious or self-aware. The book will therefore treat machine consciousness and machine self-awareness as open scientific and philosophical questions, not current facts. Superintelligence (SI) means a hypothetical future system substantially exceeding human capability across many consequential cognitive domains.

Forecast labels: OBSERVED; PROJECTED; POLYMATH FORECAST; SCENARIO; SPECULATIVE HORIZON; NORMATIVE PROPOSAL.

Source rule: historical and contemporary factual claims require sources. External forecasts require attribution. Polymath forecasts must expose assumptions, causal chain, counterforces, signposts, confidence, and alternative paths.

## Recurring Human Role Framework

Every future-facing chapter should answer six questions:
1. What can the machine plausibly do?
2. What should humans still control?
3. What might humans choose to preserve even if automation is possible?
4. What new human capability becomes more valuable because machines exist?
5. Who gains power, wealth, time or freedom, and who could lose them?
6. What design would make this transition enlarge human agency?

The intended endpoint is not a civilization with no role for humans. It is a civilization in which survival and dignity are progressively less dependent on performing tasks machines can perform, leaving humans more room for relationships, purpose, exploration, creativity, stewardship, citizenship, play, learning and freely chosen work.

## Developmental editing warning

Completeness does not mean maximal length. This architecture deliberately over-captures the subject. During drafting, overlapping chapters should be merged where that improves narrative flow. The final book should feel like one argument unfolding, not an encyclopedia assembled from disconnected essays.
