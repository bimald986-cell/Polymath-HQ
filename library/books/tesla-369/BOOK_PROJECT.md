# Book Project: 3, 6, 9 — Tesla, Mathematics, Myth, and Meaning

Status: Research/outline approved for future manuscript development
Created: 2026-10-02

## Purpose
Create an original, evidence-aware popular-science/philosophy book about the cultural idea commonly called “Tesla’s 3-6-9 theory.” The book must clearly distinguish documented Nikola Tesla history from later internet mythology, mathematical facts, numerology, philosophical interpretation, and modern manifestation practices.

## Editorial rule
Never state that Nikola Tesla said “If you only knew the magnificence of the 3, 6 and 9...” or “If you want to find the secrets of the universe, think in terms of energy, frequency and vibration” as established quotations unless a verifiable primary source is found. Current research has not established a primary Tesla source for either quotation.

Do not present numerology, vortex mathematics, manifestation, sacred geometry, frequency healing, or similar claims as established physics. They may be explored as history, culture, philosophy, mathematical pattern-making, or personal reflective practice with clear labels.

## Working title
**3 • 6 • 9: Tesla, Mathematics, Myth, and the Human Search for Hidden Order**

Alternative commercial title:
**The Mystery of 3, 6 & 9: What Tesla Really Knew, What Mathematics Reveals, and Why These Numbers Fascinate Us**

## Central promise
A curious reader should finish the book understanding:
1. who Tesla actually was;
2. what he really worked on: AC systems, high-frequency currents, oscillation, resonance, wireless transmission and invention;
3. what can and cannot be historically attributed to him regarding 3, 6 and 9;
4. why multiples of 3 and 9 produce striking decimal/digital-root patterns;
5. the difference between mathematical identities and physical laws;
6. how 3, 6 and 9 appear in culture, symbolism and numerology;
7. how the modern 369 manifestation method developed;
8. why humans perceive patterns and attach meaning to them;
9. how to remain open-minded without confusing possibility with evidence;
10. how to use 3-6-9 as a harmless reflective framework without claiming supernatural certainty.
