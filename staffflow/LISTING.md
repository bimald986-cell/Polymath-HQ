# StaffFlow — Intent-to-Return Tracker (listing copy)

## What it does

One Excel workbook that answers the question every international school faces between
January and March: **who is coming back next year?**

- A **Staff register** with one row per staff member — contract type, contract end date,
  nationality, line manager — and an **intent dropdown**: Returning / Undecided /
  Not returning / No response.
- A **live Summary**: counts by intent, counts by department, percent responded, and
  forecast vacancies (confirmed, and worst case). Every number is a formula, so it
  updates the moment you change a row.
- A **Follow-ups** sheet that lists only the people who have not answered, with a
  follow-up due date worked out from the date the answer was chased, and overdue rows
  highlighted in red.
- A **Start here** sheet with six steps and a list of what not to edit, and a **Lists**
  sheet holding the dropdown options and the follow-up window (14 days by default).

Delivered as one `.xlsx` file. Opens in Excel (2016 or later), Excel for the web, and
Google Sheets — upload to Drive and it converts. No macros, no add-ins, no login.

## Who it is for

The HR admin, registrar or principal's secretary at a Gulf international school
(30–300 staff) who currently tracks resignations in a shared spreadsheet, a WhatsApp
group and their memory. If you need a defensible number for "how many teachers are
leaving?" before a board meeting or a hiring trip, this is that number.

Works for a single school on a single campus. Also fine for a school group that wants one
file per school.

## What it does not do

- It does not decide anything for you. It records what staff told you and does the counting.
- It does not send email, reminders or messages. Nothing is automated outside the workbook.
- It does not connect to your HRIS, payroll or Google Workspace.
- It does not store documents, contracts, visas or passport copies — it holds a
  nationality field and contract dates, nothing more.
- It is not a compliance product. It does not implement GDPR, UAE PDPL or any other
  regime; keeping the file on the school's own drive under its normal HR access rules is
  your responsibility.
- It does not forecast by department headcount planning, budget or salary. "Forecast
  vacancies" is a count of people, not a cost.
- It does not work in Excel 2013 or older, and it does not work in Numbers.
