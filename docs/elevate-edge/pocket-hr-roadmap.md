# Pocket HR — Product roadmap (future)

Software path for Elevate Edge. **Do not build full product until service revenue and demand are proven.**

## Vision

Mobile-friendly web (then app): day-to-day career/HR partner for individuals; later light tools for managers; optional job board much later.

## Phase 1 — MVP

- Multiple CV templates (4–6 quality templates)
- Structured CV editor + PDF export
- Role-based bullet suggestions (user-editable)
- Job/role explainer library
- Scoped HR/career Q&A + disclaimers
- User accounts, save CVs

## Phase 2 — Daily partner

- Interview packs by role
- JD decoder / cover letter from pasted JD
- Review phrase banks, onboarding checklists
- Personal HR notebook

## Phase 3 — Light employer

- JD generator for managers
- Interview scorecards

## Phase 4 — Job board (optional)

Only with traffic and trust. Two-sided marketplace is a separate growth problem.

## Business model (draft)

Free tier (1 CV, limits) · Pro subscription · optional one-time packs · later Team plan

## Principle

Service brand (**Elevate Edge**) funds learning; product (**Pocket HR**) productizes what clients already pay for.
