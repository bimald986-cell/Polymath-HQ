# Elevate Edge — Career Service

Venture board inside **Polymath-HQ**.  
Service brand for CV/resume, LinkedIn, interview prep, and career guidance.  
Long-term product path: **Pocket HR** (see [pocket-hr-roadmap.md](pocket-hr-roadmap.md)).

**Status:** Start small (manual services). Scale in Nepal later.

---

## Mission

Empowering job seekers to present their strongest professional story and move toward roles that fit them.

**Tagline options**

- Career materials that get you shortlisted.
- Present your best self—clearly.

**Positioning:** HR-informed career service (not cheap CV typing).

---

## Service pillars

1. CV / Resume creation & rewrite  
2. LinkedIn profile optimization  
3. Portfolio design (where relevant)  
4. Career coaching & interview prep  

---

## Packages

| Tier | Includes | Price guide | Delivery |
|------|----------|-------------|----------|
| **Edge Start** | 1-page CV rewrite or strong edit + 1 revision | $49–89 | 5–7 days |
| **Edge Pro** | CV + LinkedIn + 1 revision each | $99–179 | 7–10 days |
| **Edge Career** | CV + LinkedIn + 60-min interview prep + 7-day email support | $179–299 | 10–14 days |
| **Add-on** | Portfolio lite / rush / extra revision | $15–99 | — |

**Rules:** Intake before work; one revision included; no invented experience; no job guarantee.

